How Deskclerk approaches AI use

  • Humans stay in the loop. Every draft is a starting point, not a final answer — you review and approve before anything goes out.
  • Keep sensitive data out. Avoid pasting confidential, personal, or proprietary information you wouldn't want stored or processed by a third-party model.
  • Sources are shown, not hidden. Research answers link back to where the information came from so you can check it yourself.
  • Outputs can be wrong. Treat summaries, plans, and facts as a first pass — verify anything that matters before you rely on it.
